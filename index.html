01 / ABOUT

THE PERSON BEHIND THE NAME

Who is ASHVTM?

ASHVTM is the stage name of Adesh Meshram, an independent creative artist from Amravati, Maharashtra, India.

ASHVTM is a creative identity built around music, unique storytelling and travelling. The aim is to explore ideas, experiences and emotions, then share them through sound and visuals.

✦

A quick clarification: ASHVTM is Adesh Meshram’s stage name. The name is not connected to Ashwatthama.
